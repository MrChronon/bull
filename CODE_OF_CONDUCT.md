# BULL Community Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_RU.md)

## Our standard

BULL welcomes technical disagreement and rigorous criticism. We expect everyone
to discuss ideas, evidence and trade-offs without attacking people. English and
Russian contributions are equally welcome.

Positive participation includes:

- being specific, reproducible and open about uncertainty;
- giving constructive feedback and acknowledging corrections;
- respecting different experience levels, hardware budgets and language skills;
- protecting private data and reporting security concerns responsibly;
- separating benchmark evidence from claims about people or communities.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, spam and personal
attacks.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in Issues, Discussions, pull requests and other spaces
managed by the project. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.

Use GitHub's built-in **Report content** flow for abuse or harassment. Do not
publish personal details in an Issue. Security vulnerabilities follow
[the security policy](SECURITY.md), not the public conduct process.
